Darenda — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Darenda is an app by Nuvqetra LLC for finding apartments in Da Nang, Vietnam. For privacy questions, contact support@nuvqetra.com.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

You do not need an account to use Darenda. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, user tracking, or purchases.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, filters, display preferences, saved listings, and cached images are stored on your device. Searching and filtering take place on your device. The app does not send your search text or filter settings to the Darenda API.

You can remove favorites in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; backups created by iOS are managed through your Apple settings.

Loading listings

To retrieve listings, photos, and videos, the app connects to darenda.app and media hosting servers. A request for an individual listing includes its identifier. During a network connection, servers and infrastructure providers receive technical information needed to deliver a response, including your IP address. This is separate from the local storage of favorites and search activity.

To protect the API against excessive requests, your IP address is briefly used in an in-memory rate-limit counter. The counter uses a one-minute window and is removed when expired windows are cleared, normally within approximately two minutes. It is not written to a user profile database. Darenda does not use these requests to build a search history or advertising profile.

Location and maps

Darenda uses Apple Maps. Access to your location is requested only when you use the feature that shows your current location. Permission is optional: you can browse listings and the map of Da Nang without granting it.

The app does not send your location to the Darenda API. Apple's system map and location services process information under Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

You can change location permission in the iOS settings for Darenda.

Translating descriptions

When you request a translation, Apple's system translation service translates the description on your device. Darenda does not send the description to its own API for translation. An internet connection may be needed to download language models initially. Apple may collect API usage and performance metrics, including the app identifier and the source and target languages, but not the original or translated text. Use of this system service is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

External links and sharing listings

Contact buttons open Telegram, WhatsApp, Zalo, or external pages. Further processing of information is governed by the relevant service's policies. Darenda does not receive your conversations with agents. This policy describes the native iOS app. Websites you visit may use their own technologies, including web analytics; these are not embedded in the app's native screens.

When you choose to share, a link to a listing or a selection of listings is passed to the app you select. A link to a selection contains the identifiers of the selected listings; anyone who receives the link can view that selection on the Darenda website.

Contacting support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message, and use them to respond to your request. To ask about deleting this correspondence, contact support@nuvqetra.com.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app works changes, we will update this page and indicate the date of the revised policy.
