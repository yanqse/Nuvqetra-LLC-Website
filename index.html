Privacy Policy · last updated June 15, 2026

In short: Dots collects no data. Everything stays on your device only.

Data we collect

None. All your data (habits, check-ins, birth date, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to it.

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs or ads.
  • No accounts or servers.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications on your device. They never leave the device.

Photos access

The app requests Photos access only to save a progress card you chose to save. We do not read your photo library.

Widgets

Widgets display your own on-device data (via a shared App Group container). This data never leaves your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal data, including from children.
