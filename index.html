Meet Dots Company details

What we do

We are a product company, not an agency. Everything we ship is our own — designed, written and supported in-house.

Native applications

We build for Apple platforms with SwiftUI — apps that feel like they belong on the device rather than a web page in a wrapper.

Long-term products

We keep our apps for years, not for a launch. Features are added carefully and removed when they stop earning their place.

Privacy as a default

Our apps work on your device first. Accounts and synchronisation are optional, and we have no interest in collecting more than a feature needs.

Direct support

Questions are answered by the people who wrote the code. There is no ticket queue and no outsourced support desk.

Our product

One app, made properly, rather than a catalogue of half-finished ones.

Dots

An app for habits, tasks and a calm view of your own time. It keeps the day in one quiet list and shows the larger shape around it: the year as a grid of days, a habit as a field that slowly fills in, the weeks of your life as they pass.

Dots runs on iPhone and Mac, speaks eight languages, and works entirely offline if that is what you want.

About Dots

About the company

Nuvqetra LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, United States, and formed in August 2026.

The company was set up around a single idea: build software we would want to use ourselves, sell it directly to the people who use it, and keep it going for years rather than chasing a launch. There are no investors to satisfy and no advertising business to feed, which is why our apps have no ads, no trackers and no growth tricks inside them.

We publish through the Apple App Store, support our users by email, and handle design, engineering and support in-house.
